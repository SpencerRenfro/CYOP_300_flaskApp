Steam Action

Steam Action is a carpet cleaning company that my father started. I created a website for him to use to manage his business and to get new customers. The website is built using Tailwind, and React.js. It is a single page application or SPA for short. I incorporated a cost estimate calculator to help customers get a ball park estimate of the cost to clean their carpets.
